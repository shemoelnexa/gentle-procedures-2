## Site-wide rules
1. **No valet.** There is no valet and never was. Remove every mention (12 on the staging build:
   contact-us ×4, what-to-expect ×3, faqs ×2, index ×2, whats-included ×1).
2. **No district.** Address is `Shop 7, Al Ferdous Building 3, Al Wasl Road, Dubai` — no
   "Al Safa 1", no "Jumeirah", anywhere, footer included. Address links open Google Maps.
3. **Dashes.** House style uses commas, colons, full stops or brackets. Em/en dashes go
   site-wide (~175 on the staging build). Where a section is marked "Punctuation only",
   that is the whole of the change.
4. **British spelling** throughout. `pediatric` → `paediatric` (our-team.html ×2).
5. **Always "Gentle Procedures Clinic"**, never "Gentle Procedures Dubai".
6. **No "doesn't end, in many ways it begins"** constructions — removed across the site.
7. Deliberate exceptions to house style, keep as-is: **RTA parking**, **Emirates ID card**.
