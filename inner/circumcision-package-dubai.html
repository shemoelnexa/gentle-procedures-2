01–03

Before your visit

  1. Your questions answered before you ask — by message, in English or Arabic, at any hour.
  2. Your son's preparation confirmed individually, in writing.
  3. Rescheduling that is free, simple, and guilt-free.

04–12

The day itself

  1. The valet at the door.
  2. A welcome by name.
  3. The lounge and the play area, for as long as you need them.
  4. A nurse who explains every detail before anything happens.
  5. A private room for the procedure — with you beside him, if you wish.
  6. The Pollock Technique, performed by a surgeon trained personally by Dr Neil Pollock.
  7. A soft embroidered blanket of his own, to wrap him the moment it's done.
  8. A private room afterwards, for feeding and quiet.
  9. An unhurried aftercare walk-through, in plain words.

13–18

After you leave

  1. The discharge pack: everything you'll need — and a few small extras from us.
  2. Your guidance three ways: printed, WhatsApp, email.
  3. Check-ins through the first days.
  4. Day four: your photographs, reviewed personally by your surgeon.
  5. A team that is easy to reach whenever you're worried.
  6. If anything ever worries you enough to want us to look — we look. That's included too.
