How we hire

Warmth
first.

Families rarely remember the details of a visit. They remember how it felt — and the people who made it feel that way. So we hire for warmth first, and train everything else to the same standard.

Every person on this page was chosen with the same care we promise your son. Everyone here will know your name, your son's name, and quite possibly your coffee order.

Who does what

Four people, and
not one stranger.

A small team who do one thing only: care for newborns through a gentle circumcision.

01Before the day

The person who answers your questions before the day knows this care inside out — and will still be here when you arrive.

02Your nurse

The nurse who stays with your son throughout is experienced in exactly this, chosen as much for calm and warmth as for skill, and she'll often notice the small cues before parents do.

03Your surgeon

Your surgeon gives the procedure his complete attention, because it's the only one he does. A consultant paediatric surgeon, devoted to a single craft — you'll meet him properly, and unhurried, before anything begins.

He trained personally and extensively with Dr Neil Pollock in Vancouver, and brings the Pollock Technique to the UAE for the first time. There is a fuller story, and we look forward to sharing it soon.

04Your own room

And you wait with your son in your own private room rather than a shared waiting area.
