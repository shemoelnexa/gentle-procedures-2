Why

Every decision in this clinic — the technique we chose, the people we hired, the way your time with us unfolds — exists in service of a single craft: newborn circumcision, done as gently as we know how. This page explains how, and why.

The Technique

The Pollock
Technique™

The Pollock Technique™ is the heart of everything we do — a method developed and refined over decades by Dr Neil Pollock of Vancouver, carrying the experience of more than 100,000 families. It is practised today in Gentle Procedures clinics across Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia.

Your surgeon trained personally and extensively with Dr Pollock, and now brings the technique home to the UAE.

Our Philosophy

Gentle is not a word we use lightly here — it is a standard we practise. It means your baby is wrapped warm and spoken to softly. It means you are welcome beside him the whole way through. It means the room is calm because everyone in it knows exactly what they are doing. And it means we measure ourselves: every family's experience is reviewed by the whole team, and the smallest improvement is treated as worth making. Gentleness, for us, is not a promise made once on a website. It is a practice, repeated every day.

How We Improve

Measured,
not asserted.

Gentle is a word any clinic can use. We decided ours should be measured, not asserted — and improved, deliberately, every week.

Gentle Evolution is our team's weekly discipline: everyone here captures what they notice — a small friction in a family's visit, a moment that worked beautifully, an idea worth trying — and every week we decide together what to improve, and make the change.

Small, constant, compounding.
