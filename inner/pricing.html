Before you ask

The questions worth asking anywhere — including of us.

What does the fee include, completely?

One fee covers everything: your son's procedure with the Pollock Technique, the whole of your family's visit, the discharge pack, the day-four photograph review, and the care that follows at home. If we do it, it's included. Nothing is added on the day, and we only take payment once we're all set to go ahead. If the appointment ever needs to move, or your son is referred onward for specialist care, you're never out of pocket.

Is circumcision covered by insurance in the UAE?

It depends on your policy. When circumcision is chosen for religious, cultural or personal reasons, as it is for most of the families we welcome, UAE insurers generally class it as an elective procedure, and most policies don't cover it. Where there's a specific medical reason, some policies do, so it's worth checking directly with your insurer before the day. We don't bill insurers directly. Your fee is settled with us at the clinic, and if your policy allows you to claim it back, we'll gladly prepare everything you need: a receipt, a procedure summary, and any paperwork your insurer asks for.

What if I need to reschedule at the last minute?

Please don't worry. There's no deposit and no cancellation fee. Just let us know as soon as you can and we'll find another time that suits you. If your son is unwell on the day, a temperature, off his feeds, not himself, rescheduling is exactly the right thing to do, and we'd always rather you did.
