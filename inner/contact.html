Arriving

Arriving is the part parents quietly dread. So we took it off your hands.

The valet, and the door

A short service road off Al Wasl Road brings you to our front door, where we'll meet you and take care of your car. If you'd rather park yourself, the parking is beneath the building, entered from the rear.

What we send you beforehand

We'll send you a map pin, clear directions and a plan for the day, including when to feed him and when to set off.

Give yourself plenty of time

Dubai traffic is unpredictable, and arriving unhurried makes the whole morning calmer. Bring your Emirates ID, and your son's if it's ready.
