Aftercare guide

Caring for him at home.

This is the public version of the guidance every family receives from us in full: printed, on WhatsApp and by email, with every step confirmed for your own son.

As the numbing wears off, many babies have a harder evening. It doesn’t mean something is wrong, and it passes. Keep the evening low and slow: feeds, swaddling, skin-to-skin, a quiet room. His comfort medicine plan is in your pack.

Vaseline is the whole job. Generous, at every change, for the full two weeks. From day three it matters more, not less, even though he’ll seem better. Most of the worried messages we receive begin with the Vaseline quietly stopping too soon.

Bathing is part of the healing, from the point we show you. Your pack has the exact steps for your son. The habit that matters is the daily soak, kept up for the full fortnight.

Healing has its own appearance. Swelling that seems generous. A soft white or yellow film that looks alarming, and is in fact a good sign. What you see in week one is nowhere near the final result.

When to contact us straight away

Some things need plain words: bleeding that keeps coming, a fever, no wet nappies, redness that is spreading, or a baby who simply seems unwell to you. Contact us straight away. If he ever seems seriously unwell, hard to wake, struggling to breathe, call 998 first. Then call us.
