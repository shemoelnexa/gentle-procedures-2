Arriving

Where to park.

Parking is free beneath the building, entered from the rear. If you would rather stay at street level, there is RTA parking directly outside.

A short service road off Al Wasl Road brings you to our front door.

What we send you beforehand

We’ll send you a map pin, clear directions, the parking instructions and a plan for the day, including when to feed him and when to set off. Give yourself plenty of time. Dubai traffic is unpredictable, and arriving unhurried makes the whole morning calmer. Bring your Emirates ID card, and your son’s if it’s ready.
